CONTINUITY

The line belongs.

Every strand enters a real hand, instrument, fold or body contour. The endpoint follows the actual joint. The two battle ropes terminate in separate hands; the kite tether follows the rotating diamond.

CHOREOGRAPHY

Actions, not speeds.

Listening repeats. Finishing damps, winds, combs, folds or catches. Preview has a held pose. Insertion anticipates, releases and follows through once. Replay lets you examine that finite gesture.

TRUST

Words before whimsy.

Live text sits above the moving line. Final modified text appears above a straight line before insertion. Red dotted uncertainty remains in both surfaces. A held error retains the sentence and pauses delivery.

RESTRAINT

Use the whole bay.

The character uses the height of the notch, with headroom for ears, hats and expressive gestures. Idle keeps the green dot; ready keeps the Right Control hint. Native-size preview checks the balance against readable text.
