Signature mark + pill — 22 concepts

The product name is tentative, so nothing here depends on it. Each concept is a mark (app icon, menu-bar glyph) and a pill behaviour (the same mark, alive). Every stage below is live: the pill hangs under the notch exactly where it does today, and the field beneath it is the app the words land in. Pick one, then DESIGN.md specifies it.
