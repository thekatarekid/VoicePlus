The character earns its place by telling you what the product is doing.

01 / Recognise the outline

A silhouette first.

Long ears. A segmented shell. A paper tongue. The shape should still belong to the character when its eyes disappear.

02 / Respect the pause

Waiting is a gesture.

A raised ear, a held page, a half-open pocket. Thinking must look different from finishing.

03 / Make privacy physical

Close something real.

A shell seals. A hood cinches. A shutter slides. Muting changes the anatomy, not just the colour.

04 / Give it one job

Deliver, then rest.

No dialogue, pet-care chores, or attention-seeking. One small gesture at the moment your words become yours.

All names are working creative directions; availability has not been checked. The 16 px views are silhouette studies, not finished icon assets. Audio recognition, insertion into other apps, and privacy controls are simulated here. Idle and muted characters are deliberately still; previews stop rendering offscreen or when paused. All artwork is drawn as animated vector geometry inside this single file.
