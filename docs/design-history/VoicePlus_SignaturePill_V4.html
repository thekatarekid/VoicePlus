Placement is part of the identity

Give it a reason to be there.

My default direction is near the caret for deliberate dictation, or attached to the active window for longer sessions.

Near the caret

Best connection between voice and result. Keep the object below the baseline, clear of selection handles. If caret geometry is unavailable, fall back to the active window’s edge.

On the active window

A reliable home with less visual travel. Follow the focused window, not the pointer. Keep clear of traffic lights, toolbar buttons, resize handles, and the camera.

Above the Dock

A stable, independent home with room to unfold. Move out of the way when the Dock appears, and keep a small destination cue near the caret during dictation.

These are speculative interface concepts, not changes to the shipping app. Voice input, speaker recognition, insertion, and mute are simulated. The forms are enlarged for inspection; the desktop view shows proposed placement rather than a final pixel specification. At rest and muted, the objects are still. Animations stop when paused, offscreen, or in a hidden tab. Reduced-motion preferences are respected. Working names have not been checked for availability.
