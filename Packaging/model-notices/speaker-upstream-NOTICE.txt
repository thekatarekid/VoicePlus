VoicePlus bundles the legacy pyannote_segmentation.mlmodelc and wespeaker_v2.mlmodelc conversions, unchanged from FluidInference. These are not the newer Community-1 artifact set.

Credits: pyannote (Herve Bredin and contributors), WeSpeaker (Wenet community and contributors), and FluidInference for the CoreML conversion and optimization.
Original segmentation: https://huggingface.co/pyannote/segmentation-3.0 (MIT; included upstream LICENSE).
Original embedding: https://huggingface.co/pyannote/wespeaker-voxceleb-resnet34-LM (CC-BY-4.0; included upstream model card and CC-BY license text).
The historical FluidInference card identifies the 3.1/WeSpeaker lineage and labels its conversion Apache-2.0; the current NOTICE clarifies that Community-1 confirmation does not cover legacy artifacts. Both notices are retained without broadening their scope.
VoicePlus does not modify the converted model weights or claim endorsement. File integrity is recorded in each model's voiceplus-manifest.json. Notice source revisions identify documentation, not a claimed conversion build revision.
