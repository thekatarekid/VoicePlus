Both are derived from NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 (CC-BY-4.0):
https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3

Parakeet Ultra — full-precision post-training of Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 by moondream,
converted to Core ML (int8 encoder) by FluidInference.
https://huggingface.co/FluidInference/parakeet-ultra-coreml

Parakeet Redux — ternary re-training of Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 by moondream,
converted to Core ML (2-bit palettised encoder) by FluidInference.
https://huggingface.co/FluidInference/parakeet-redux-coreml

VoicePlus downloads and runs the converted weights unchanged.
