Auto-AVSR (audio-visual and visual-only speech recognition)

Code: https://github.com/mpc001/auto_avsr — Apache License 2.0.
Weights: trained on LRS3 (and, for the AV model, LRS2/VoxCeleb2/AVSpeech). The LRS3-derived weights
are licensed for NON-COMMERCIAL RESEARCH USE ONLY.

VoicePlus is a non-commercial application; using these weights was accepted by the owner (V5 LR4,
1 Oct 2026). This is VoicePlus's one non-commercial dependency. The weights are not bundled or
downloaded by the app: the user converts them locally (tools/avsr/README.md). If VoicePlus ever
becomes commercial, these weights must be removed or replaced by a model trained on licensable data.

Ma, Haliassos, Fernandez-Lopez, Chen, Petridis, Pantic. "Auto-AVSR: Audio-Visual Speech Recognition
with Automatic Labels." ICASSP 2023.
