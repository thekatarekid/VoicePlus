This is a motion prototype: voice levels, text insertion, speaker recognition, and haptics are simulated. Ambient motion and Creature’s six-second blink follow the concept notes; they are exceptions to the brief’s still-idle ambition. Still states do not animate their marks. Offscreen previews and hidden tabs stop rendering. Sound is opt-in. Halo’s no-notch fallback is included; unresolved icon treatments are labelled.
